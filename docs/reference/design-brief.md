BoraBet — Domino-Only Master UI/UX Design Prompt (Telegram Mini App, Brazil) Purpose of this document: Paste this whole file into Figma AI / your design tool as the single source of truth for visual design and UX only. It assumes the engineering, ledger, compliance, and backend rules from the original BoraBet master prompt still apply — but here we design a Domino-first, premium, modern, mobile-first Telegram Mini App with only one game: Domino 1v1. Scope for this pass: Design every screen, state, icon, illustration, and animation for a Domino-only product. No Plinko, no wheel, no tournaments of other games. Every visual asset is original. Nothing is a template. Golden rule for the designer: Every screen must feel like a premium Brazilian mobile game at 2 AM — deep navy, glowing yellow, glossy 3D icons, chunky pill buttons, chunky rounded cards, subtle idle motion only where it earns attention. If a screen could belong to any other casino, redo it. 0. Design intent in one paragraph BoraBet is a dark navy, yellow-glowing, domino-obsessed Telegram Mini App. The player opens the bot, and within two taps they are at a green-felt table with a wooden gold-trimmed border, listening to a soft Brazilian samba-adjacent UI loop, watching their own glossy 3D domino tiles slide and click into place. The entire app is built around one game, done beautifully: Domino 1v1, stake a room, winner takes the pot minus a 10% platform commission. The mascot is a stylized jaguar ("Onça") who appears on the hero banner, points the player toward the Jogar button, and celebrates wins with a tail flick. Everything else — deposit, withdraw, wallet, profile, rewards, chat, settings — exists to funnel the player back into a domino match as fast and as pleasantly as possible. Two emotional pillars: "Bora!" — speed. Everything is instant. No spinners on the lobby. No dead buttons. Tap → play in seconds. "Uma mesa, uma lenda." — one game, premium. The domino table is treated like a physical object: felt, wood, gold, ivory tiles, real sound, real weight. 1. Visual identity 1.1 Logo Wordmark: "BoraBet" in Lilita One, tight letter spacing, all caps, with the "B" slightly larger and a small yellow glow. The dot of the "i" is replaced by a tiny domino tile showing a 5-5 (the Brazilian "carreta de cinco" — the most powerful opening tile, culturally resonant). Emblem: a rounded-square badge, deep navy #0D1222, with a glossy 3D domino tile tilted 12°, gold trim, and a jaguar paw print in the corner. Used as the Telegram bot avatar and mini app icon. Favicon: the emblem, simplified, 32×32, no text. 1.2 Mascot — "Onça" the jaguar A stylized, friendly, slightly chunky jaguar. Not realistic — think premium mobile game mascot (like a Duolingo owl but a jaguar, with Brazilian warmth). Golden-yellow fur with dark navy rosettes, big expressive eyes, thick tail, small rounded ears. Six poses needed: Idle — standing, tail curled, slight breathing animation (chest rises 2px, tail sways 3°). Point-down — right paw pointing down-right toward the Jogar button (used on hero banner slide 1). Celebrate — arms up, tail straight up, confetti around (used on win screen). Surprised — eyes wide, ears back, tile held up (used on "opponent found" moment). Sad-neutral — sitting, tail wrapped around feet, no tears (used on loss screen — never shame the player). VIP — wearing a tiny gold crown and a domino tile as a monocle (used on high-tier profile and VIP rewards). Every pose exported as PNG @2x and @3x, transparent background, with a soft drop shadow baked in.
